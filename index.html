UK Design Poster Days

Degree shows and final-year project exhibitions for product, furniture, industrial, interior and design-engineering courses across the UK, all on one page. Use it to plan graduate scouting, find new talent or catch the best student work of the year.

Read this first: most universities announce their dates in spring, so the dates below are from the 2026 shows (last updated 19 Nov 2026). Each card has a 2027 guide based on when the show usually runs. Always check the official link before you travel.
Dates come from each university's or organiser's own pages and from event listings published in 2026. "Project show" means a final-year design or engineering project exhibition, usually with an industry night. Shows such as Cardiff Met's hadn't published dates when this page was compiled, so they're marked TBC.
