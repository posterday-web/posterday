About this show

Edinburgh Napier Degree Show is a final-year degree show from Edinburgh Napier University, held at Merchiston campus, 10 Colinton Road, Edinburgh. The 2026 show ran from 29 May 2026 to 5 June 2026.

It covers Graphics & Illustration, Interiors & Architecture, Product & Furniture, Photography, Film & Animation and Fine Art, with work from these courses: Design and visual arts, undergraduate and postgraduate.
