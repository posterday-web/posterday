About this show

The Degree Show: Made It is a final-year degree show from University of Derby, held at Britannia Mill and Markeaton Street, Derby. The 2026 show ran from 6 June 2026 to 12 June 2026.

It covers Fine Art, Fashion & Textiles, Interiors & Architecture, Photography, Film & Animation and Theatre & Performance Design, with work from these courses: Art & Design, Fashion, Interior Architecture, Photography, Film, Theatre.
