About this show

GradEx Creative Industries Degree Show is a final-year degree show from Staffordshire University, held at Stoke-on-Trent campus. The 2026 show ran from 29 May 2026 to 6 June 2026.

It covers Graphics & Illustration, Fine Art, Fashion & Textiles, Photography and Film & Animation, with work from these courses: Graphic Design, Illustration, Cartoon & Comic Arts, Fine Art, Fashion, Textiles, Animation, Photography.
