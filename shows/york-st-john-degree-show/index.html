About this show

York St John Degree Show is a final-year degree show from York St John University, held at Lord Mayor's Walk, York. The 2026 show ran from 4 June 2026 to 10 June 2026.

It covers Graphics & Illustration, Fine Art, Photography and Film & Animation, with work from these courses: Illustration, Fine Art, Photography.
