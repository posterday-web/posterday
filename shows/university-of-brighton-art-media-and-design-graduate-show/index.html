About this show

Art, Media and Design Graduate Show is a final-year degree show from University of Brighton, held at Grand Parade, City campus, Brighton. The 2026 show ran from 30 May 2026 to 7 June 2026.

It covers Graphics & Illustration, Fine Art, Photography, Film & Animation, Fashion & Textiles, Jewellery, Ceramics & Craft and Product & Furniture, with work from these courses: Graphic Design, Illustration, Fine Art, Photography, Fashion, Textiles, 3D Design & Craft.
