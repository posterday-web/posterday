About this show

Prologue: Interim MFA Exhibition is a final-year degree show from Belfast School of Art, Ulster University, held at Art Gallery, Belfast campus, York Street, Belfast. The 2026 show ran from 9 January 2026 to 28 January 2026.

It covers Fine Art, with work from these courses: MFA Fine Art, shown in two parts.
