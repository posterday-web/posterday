About this show

DOT DOT DOT Graduate Festival is a final-year degree show from Coventry University, held at Delia Derbyshire Building, Coventry. The 2026 show ran from 1 June 2026.

It covers Product & Furniture, Graphics & Illustration, Fine Art, Fashion & Textiles, Photography and Film & Animation, with work from these courses: Product Design, Automotive & Transport Design, Graphic Design, Illustration, Fine Art, Fashion.
