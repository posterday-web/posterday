About this show

UCA Grad Show – Canterbury is a final-year degree show from University for the Creative Arts, held at New Dover Road, Canterbury CT1. The 2026 show ran from 11 June 2026 to 24 June 2026.

It covers Interiors & Architecture, Fine Art and Graphics & Illustration, with work from these courses: Architecture, Interior Architecture, Fine Art, Graphic Design.
