About this show

Inspired Festival is a final-year degree show from Birmingham City University, held at Parkside, Margaret Street and School of Jewellery, Birmingham. The 2026 show ran from 9 June 2026 to 27 June 2026.

It covers Product & Furniture, Interiors & Architecture, Graphics & Illustration, Fashion & Textiles, Fine Art, Jewellery, Ceramics & Craft, Photography and Film & Animation, with work from these courses: Product & Furniture, Interior Design, Visual Communication, Fashion, Textiles, Fine Art, Jewellery.
