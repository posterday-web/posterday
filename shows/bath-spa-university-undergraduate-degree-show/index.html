About this show

Undergraduate Degree Show is a final-year degree show from Bath Spa University, held at Locksbrook Campus, Bath. The 2026 show ran from 6 June 2026 to 13 June 2026.

It covers Product & Furniture, Interiors & Architecture, Graphics & Illustration, Fashion & Textiles, Fine Art, Photography and Film & Animation, with work from these courses: Product Design, Interior Design, Graphic Communication, Fashion, Textiles, Fine Art, Photography, Film.
