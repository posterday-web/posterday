About this show

Heriot-Watt Masters Degree Show is a final-year degree show from Heriot-Watt University, held at Scottish Borders Campus, Galashiels. The 2026 show ran from 6 August 2026 to 7 August 2026.

It covers Fashion & Textiles, Interiors & Architecture and Graphics & Illustration, with work from these courses: Postgraduate design, fashion and textiles.
