About this show

CSAD Degree Show is a final-year degree show from Cardiff Metropolitan University, held at Llandaff Campus, Cardiff. Dates for this year have not been announced yet.

It covers Product & Furniture, Interiors & Architecture, Graphics & Illustration, Fashion & Textiles, Fine Art, Jewellery, Ceramics & Craft, Photography and Film & Animation, with work from these courses: Product Design, Interior Design, Graphic Communication, Illustration, Fashion, Textiles, Ceramics, Fine Art, Photography, Animation.
