About this show

SDCA Degree Show is a final-year degree show from Loughborough University, held at Design School, Loughborough. The 2026 show ran from 6 June 2026 to 10 June 2026.

It covers Product & Furniture, Graphics & Illustration, Fashion & Textiles and Fine Art, with work from these courses: Industrial Design, Product Design & Technology, Graphic Design, Fashion, Textile Design, Fine Art.
