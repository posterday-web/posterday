About this show

Cambridge School of Art Graduate Showcase is a final-year degree show from Anglia Ruskin University, held at Ruskin Gallery and studios, Cambridge. The 2026 show ran from 4 June 2026 to 13 June 2026.

It covers Graphics & Illustration, Fine Art, Fashion & Textiles, Photography and Film & Animation, with work from these courses: Illustration, Graphic Design, Fine Art, Fashion, Photography, Animation.
