About this show

Wimbledon MA Theatre & Performance Design + Puppetry is a final-year degree show from Wimbledon College of Arts, UAL, held at Merton Hall Road, London SW19. The 2026 show runs from 20 November 2026 to 26 November 2026.

It covers Theatre & Performance Design, with work from these courses: MA Theatre and Performance Design, MA Puppetry.
