About this show

Goldsmiths BA Media Degree Show (Elapse) is a final-year degree show from Goldsmiths, University of London, held at Professor Stuart Hall Building, New Cross, London SE14. The 2026 show ran from 18 June 2026 to 22 June 2026.

It covers Photography and Film & Animation, with work from these courses: BA Media, Communications & Cultural Studies.
