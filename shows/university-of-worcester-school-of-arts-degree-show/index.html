About this show

School of Arts Degree Show is a final-year degree show from University of Worcester, held at The Art House, Worcester. The 2026 show ran from 22 May 2026 to 27 May 2026.

It covers Fine Art, Graphics & Illustration, Photography and Film & Animation, with work from these courses: Fine Art, Graphic Design, Illustration, Game Art, MA Creative Media.
