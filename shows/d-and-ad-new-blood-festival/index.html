About this show

D&AD New Blood Festival is a national graduate showcase (D&AD, with 40+ exhibiting university courses), held at Protein Studios, 31 New Inn Yard, London EC2A. The 2026 show ran from 29 June 2026 to 1 July 2026.

It covers Graphics & Illustration, Photography and Film & Animation, with work from these courses: Graphic design, advertising, illustration, motion, UX and portfolio reviews.
