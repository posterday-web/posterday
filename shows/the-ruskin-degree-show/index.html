About this show

The Ruskin Degree Show is a final-year degree show from Ruskin School of Art, University of Oxford, held at 128 Bullingdon Road and 54 Marston Street, Oxford OX4. The 2026 show ran from 19 June 2026 to 24 June 2026.

It covers Fine Art, Photography and Film & Animation, with work from these courses: BFA and MFA Fine Art: painting, sculpture, installation, photography, printmaking, moving image.
