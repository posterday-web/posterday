About this show

University of Lancashire Degree Show is a final-year degree show from University of Lancashire (formerly UCLan), held at Hanover and Victoria Buildings, Preston PR1. The 2026 show ran from 11 June 2026 to 26 June 2026.

It covers Fine Art, Graphics & Illustration, Fashion & Textiles, Interiors & Architecture, Photography and Film & Animation, with work from these courses: Fine Art, Photography, Graphic Design, Illustration, Fashion, Textile Design, Interior Design, Animation, Games Design, Architecture.
