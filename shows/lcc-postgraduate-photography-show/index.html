About this show

LCC Postgraduate Photography Show is a final-year degree show from London College of Communication, UAL, held at Elephant and Castle, London SE1. The 2026 show ran from 5 November 2026 to 9 November 2026.

It covers Photography and Film & Animation, with work from these courses: MA Photography, MA Photojournalism & Documentary Photography.
