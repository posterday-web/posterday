About this show

School of Art, Architecture & Design Summer Show is a final-year degree show from London Metropolitan University, held at Shoreditch Campus, London E1. The 2026 show ran from 24 June 2026 to 29 June 2026.

It covers Product & Furniture, Interiors & Architecture, Graphics & Illustration, Fine Art, Fashion & Textiles, Jewellery and Ceramics & Craft, with work from these courses: Furniture & Product Design, Interior Design, Graphic Design, Fine Art, Fashion, Jewellery.
